# SDGO Mod Manager 1.16.59

## 中文

- 工厂加点页显示服务端确认的剩余普通点和 OC 点。尚未进入 OC 的机体右侧显示 0，每花一个普通点只扣左侧的余额。

- 无边框显示 Beta 将战斗渲染尺寸和窗口尺寸分开。选择 2160p 时可在较小的显示器上以 3840×2160 渲染，再等比缩到窗口内。输入坐标跟随当前渲染画布。

## English

- The factory allocation page shows the remaining normal and OC points confirmed by the server. Before OC, the right counter stays at zero and each normal allocation reduces the left counter by one.

- The native display Beta renders battle at the selected resolution independently of window size. For example, 2160p produces a 3840×2160 frame on a smaller monitor and fits it proportionally to the window. Pointer input follows the active render canvas.
