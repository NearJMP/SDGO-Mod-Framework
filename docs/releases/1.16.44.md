# SDGO Mod Manager 1.16.44

## 中文

- 机体外观正式提供 RGB 涂装与最多 20 层自由贴花，在原生涂装／纹章页面编辑，预览后由 World 确认，保存到具体机体并同步到房间和战斗。
- 贴花支持相接固定装甲的拐角与跨部件接缝贴合、拖动与快捷图层排序、复制、删除、显隐，以及可逆的局部划痕磨损。图层、属性和纹章库分页显示。
- 默认新增或换用纹章时消耗库存，服主可配置复用模式及 1–20 层上限。保存前列出所需物品与库存，同一确认重试不重复扣费；原生库存栏同步刷新。
- RGB 与贴花共用通信时排队保存，后台读取不再吞掉编辑操作。更新保留旧颜色、原生三个槽位的位置和独立开关，并迁移停用旧组件。
- 通信组件配套更新，保持原生消息载体大小，完整传输 20 层数据。开发 GameTest 增加原生外观页面与镜头观察入口。

## English

- Unit appearance graduates to Stable with native-page RGB paint and up to 20 independent surface decal layers. Preview, confirm with World, and share each owned unit's saved appearance in rooms and battles.
- Decals follow corners and touching seams between verified fixed armor parts, with draggable and command-based layer ordering, duplication, deletion, visibility and reversible local scratched wear. Layers, Properties and the owned library share a tabbed panel.
- New or replaced stickers consume inventory by default. Operators can enable reusable designs and a 1–20 layer limit. Quotes list required items and stock; confirmation retries never charge twice, and native inventory tiles refresh after a save.
- RGB and decal saves queue behind shared communication reads. Background refresh no longer drops editing gestures. Updates retain legacy colors, three-slot placements and independent switches while retiring duplicate components.
- Matching communication helpers carry complete twenty-layer designs without changing the native envelope size. Developer GameTest gains native appearance-page and camera observations.
