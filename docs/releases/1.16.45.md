# SDGO Mod Manager 1.16.45

## 中文

- “RGB 取色”按钮随原生右侧面板进出，文字、背景与点击区域保持对齐，修复动画中的文字漂移；原生模式入口与道具栏分页边框留有间距。
- 整备室涂装默认打开原生色卡，通过“RGB 取色”按钮进入 RGB；返回或重进页面恢复原生操作，服务器回包不再强制切换页面。
- 修复能天使等机体含有非绘制辅助网格时，自由贴花持续提示“机体画面未就绪”、无法放置的问题。
- 贴花放置直接处理鼠标点击和拖动，在模型加载或预览重建后接管左键，保留右键转动；放置失败时显示具体原因。
- 机体外观客户端 0.2.6 修复自由贴花面板能打开但标签、添加、取消等按钮点击无响应的问题，自绘按钮直接处理鼠标点击，避免固定宽度原生背景越界重叠。

## English

- The RGB entry button follows the native right panel during entrance and exit animations, keeping its caption, background and hit area aligned, with clearance below the native inventory pagination frame.
- Factory paint opens the native palette by default. Enter RGB through its button; returning or reopening restores native controls, and server replies no longer force a mode change.
- Fix decal placement remaining unavailable on models such as Gundam Exia that contain non-rendered helper meshes.
- Decal placement handles mouse clicks and drags directly, acquires left-button input during model loading and preview replacement, preserves right-button orbit and reports failed placement reasons.
- Unit appearance client 0.2.6 fixes the surface decal editor opening with unresponsive tabs, Add, Cancel and other buttons, and handles custom button clicks directly without overlapping fixed-width native backgrounds.
